Headlands Technologies — Quantitative Researcher

Headlands Technologies is a research-led proprietary trading firm. We build and
operate a large portfolio of fully automated, systematic electronic trading
strategies across futures, equities, and other delta-one products. Every trade
we make is generated by our own software — there are no discretionary
overrides on live strategies.

As a Quantitative Researcher you will:
- Design, backtest, and productionize predictive signals and trading models
  using historical and live market data (order book, trades, quotes).
- Own a research idea from hypothesis through simulation to measurable
  strategy PnL improvement in production.
- Apply statistics and machine learning to noisy, high-frequency,
  real-world market data.
- Work closely with the trading and engineering teams to deploy strategies
  that are profitable, scalable, and maintainable.
- Monitor live strategy performance, diagnose PnL and execution issues, and
  iterate on production research code.

What we look for:
- Strong programming ability in Python, C++, or Java.
- Deep understanding of statistics, probability, and applied machine
  learning on noisy data.
- Comfort with market microstructure: order books, quoting, execution,
  fills, adverse selection.
- Experience or coursework in linear algebra, optimization, and numerical
  methods.
- A systematic, data-driven mindset and the judgment to know when a
  backtest result is meaningful versus overfit.

Interview process includes technical coding rounds (data structures,
algorithms), a quantitative/statistics round, and a research case study
involving a real dataset.
