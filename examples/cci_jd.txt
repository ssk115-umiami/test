Castleton Commodities International — Quantitative Analyst, Power & Energy

CCI is a global merchant energy company trading physical and financial power,
natural gas, and other commodities. We are hiring a Quantitative Analyst to
support our power and gas trading desks.

Responsibilities:
- Build statistical and fundamental models of power and natural gas prices,
  including day-ahead/real-time price relationships, basis, and regional
  spreads.
- Work with traders to translate market and physical-grid intuition into
  quantitative tools that support trading and risk decisions.
- Analyze load, weather, generation, and outage data to understand drivers
  of price dislocations.
- Build and maintain data pipelines that combine ISO market data, weather
  data, and fundamental supply/demand data.
- Communicate model assumptions, limitations, and results clearly to
  traders and risk managers.

Qualifications:
- Strong foundation in statistics and applied mathematics; comfort with
  regression, regularization (ridge/lasso), and time-series methods.
- Understanding of linear algebra sufficient to reason about conditioning,
  multicollinearity, and model stability.
- Proficiency in Python and SQL; comfortable working with large
  dataframes.
- Interest in physical commodity markets and willingness to develop deep
  fundamental market knowledge (grid operations, pipeline flows, weather
  drivers of demand).
- Prior exposure to power or gas markets a plus but not required.

Interview process: case study built around real market data, statistics
and dataframe manipulation exercises, and a discussion of a candidate's
prior quantitative project.
